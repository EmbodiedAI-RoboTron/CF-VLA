# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and collaborate professionally.
- Focus on technical issues, not personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated disruptive behavior in issues/PRs/discussions.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates this code and
may restrict participation when necessary.
